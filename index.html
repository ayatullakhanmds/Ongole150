150 Years Municipal Legacy

Established as a municipality by the British Government on September 30, 1876, Ongole has evolved into a thriving Municipal Corporation.

World-Famous Ongole Bull

Renowned globally for strength and disease resistance, the legendary indigenous Ongole cattle breed remains the proud symbol of the city.

Andhra Kesari Heritage

Home of the legendary freedom fighter Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu (Andhra Kesari), who led with unmatched courage and determination.

Ashoka Dharma Chakra

A striking 25-foot single-stone Ashoka Dharma Chakra tower unveiled at Church Junction stands as a lasting symbol of pride and peace.
