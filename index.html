Historical Timeline (1876 - 2026)

Key chapters tracking the evolution of Ongole over 150 years.

September 30, 1876

Official establishment of Ongole Municipality under British administration, setting up localized municipal governance.

1921 - Freedom Movement

Active participation in the Non-Cooperation movement under the charismatic leadership of Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu.

1970 - District Headquarters

Ongole becomes the headquarters of the newly formed Prakasam District, accelerating regional administrative importance.

2022 - Municipal Corporation

Upgraded officially to a Municipal Corporation status, ushering in modern urban planning and expansions.

2026 - Sesquicentennial Milestone

About the Sesquicentennial

Honoring 150 years of collective progress and cultural pride.

The **Ongole 150 Years Sesquicentennial Celebrations** commemorate the official establishment of the municipality dating back to September 30, 1876. Over the past century and a half, Ongole has grown from a historic trading hub into a thriving municipal corporation in Andhra Pradesh.

This digital portal serves as an interactive archive, recording our rich history, honoring our freedom fighters like Andhra Kesari, celebrating our world-famous cattle, and keeping citizens connected with ongoing anniversary events.

Managed under the auspices of the Ongole Municipal Corporation.
